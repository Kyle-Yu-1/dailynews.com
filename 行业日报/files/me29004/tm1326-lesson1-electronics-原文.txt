### 第 11 页 · Type of Electric Motor
A motor is a device that converts electrical energy into mechanical energy. This conversion is achieved through the principles of electromagnetic induction.
1. Direct Current (DC) Motors: convert direct current electrical energy into mechanical energy. Used in toys, electric vehicles, home appliances.
2. Servo Motors: can be controlled with great precision. Used in robotics and CNC machinery.
3. Stepper Motors: move in discrete steps, allowing precise positioning. Used in printers, scanners, CNC machines.
