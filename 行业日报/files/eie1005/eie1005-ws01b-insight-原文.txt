1.4 - Workshop 01 (B) - Pre-submission Checklist：

Chart Layout：
(Q1) How Many Chart that you want to display? 1 Chart, 2 Chart, 3 Chart, 4 Chart（Sub Chart）
(Q2) What story does this data set tell?
(Q3) What Type of Chart?（Line, Area, Bar, Pie）
(Q4) What Kind of Style?（Title, Label, Legend, Grid Line, Color）

1. Data Accuracy & Integrity
 [ ] Source Consistency: Does your data match the 2023 PolyU SAO dataset?
2. Chart Formatting & Labels
 [ ] Descriptive Titles: Does every chart have a clear, bold title at the top?
 [ ] Axis Labels: Are both X and Y axes labeled with units?
 [ ] Legend Clarity: Is the legend visible and accurate?
 [ ] Scale & Intervals: Are the axis scales logical and easy to read?
3. Visual Presentation & Layout
 [ ] Consistency: Are the same colors used for the same categories across different charts?
 [ ] Readability: Are the Department names (left axis of the bar chart) fully visible and not cut off?
 [ ] Alignment: Are the three charts aligned neatly on the page with balanced white space?
 [ ] No Overlaps: Ensure data labels or text boxes are not overlapping with chart lines or bars.
