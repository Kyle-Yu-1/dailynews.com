### 第 25 页 · NVIDIA Cosmos 3
The frontier foundation model for Physical AI – breakthrough architecture; more accurate across capabilities.

Reasoner (Autoregressive) + Generator (Diffusion) · Built on Mixture of Transformer
First omnimodel to understand and generate worlds and action for physical AI.
四种角色：
- As Vision Language Model (VLM): turns vision into dense captions, sub-second insights, and alerts.
- As World Model: speeds up synthetic data generation by up to 60x – including unseen scenarios.
- As World Simulator: reduces training and evaluation from months to days.
- As World Action Model (WAM): adapts policies faster from general embodiment pretraining.
