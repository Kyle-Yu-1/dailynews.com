Welcome to the ADA interactive
playground. This platform developed by
the Hong Kong Polytenic University
simplifies the process of training deep
learning models. It covers the entire
workflow including data processing,
model creation, training, and result
evaluation. Whether you are new to deep
learning or an experienced developer,
this tool helps you explore and
understand deep learning more deeply. If
you are a beginner, you can use
preconfigured model templates without
any programming knowledge and customized
training parameters easily online. For
programmers, there is no need to invest
in expensive hardware since you can
train models directly through the online
platform. You can also tailor models to
meet your specific needs. The platform
offers a variety of built-in data sets
for experiments, research, and practice.
This saves you time by eliminating the
need to organize and clean data
manually. It provides powerful and
user-friendly deep learning functions.
You can customize model templates and
training parameters based on your goals
and different training scenarios.
Traditionally, training deep learning
models required high-end hardware and
strict environments. Now, you can train
models online conveniently without these
constraints. The cloud-based system
includes security measures to protect
your data sets. Visual evaluation tools
help you understand and communicate
complex results easily. The interface is
designed to be intuitive, allowing users
to quickly learn and navigate the
functions. To support the deep learning
process, the platform organizes features
into five main menus: introduction, data
set, model, training, and evaluation.
The introduction menu gives a brief
overview of each main menu to help you
understand their functions quickly. The
data set menu shows various built-in
data sets. You can click on each to see
its source and detailed information.
Choose the data set that best fits your
training needs. The model menu has two
parts. The first is the model editing
submen where you can create and
customize training model templates. The
second is the my model submen where you
can review, edit, or delete your saved
templates. The training menu lets you
set training parameters and start the
model training process. You can select
different data sets and model templates,
then adjust parameters to suit your
requirements. Training means teaching
the model to learn patterns from data so
it can make predictions or decisions.
The evaluation menu displays detailed
results from your training sessions.
This helps you understand how well your
model performs and make improvements
accordingly. In the future, we plan to
add more data sets, develop specialized
learning models, and support various
training scenarios. To assist users, we
provide AI customer service and feedback
channels where you can ask questions or
report issues. Our support team will
respond promptly to ensure a smooth
experience.
